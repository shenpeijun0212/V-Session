Question: Mitchell is trying to chew as many pieces of gum at once as he can. He has 8 packets of gum, There are 7 pieces in each. If he chews all the gum except for 2 pieces, how many pieces does he chew at once?
1.First, let's find the total number of gum pieces.
Mitchell has 8 packets, and each packet has 7 pieces.
So, total pieces = 8 * 7 = 56 pieces.
2.He chews all the gum except for 2 pieces.
Therefore, the number of pieces he chews = Total pieces - 2 = 56 - 2 = 54 pieces.
Conclusion: Mitchell chews 54 pieces at once.

Question: Fred has 38 red marbles, half as many green ones, and the rest are dark blue. If he has 63 marbles, how many of them are dark blue?
1.Fred has 38 red marbles.
He has half as many green marbles as red marbles.
So, green marbles = 38 / 2 = 19.
2.The total number of marbles is 63.
To find the dark blue marbles, subtract the red and green marbles from the total.
Dark blue marbles = 63 - 38 - 19 = 6.
Conclusion: Fred has 6 dark blue marbles.

Question: There are 92 students altogether. Twenty of them ride the school bus home together, 5/8 of the remaining ride their own bike home, and the rest whose houses are near the school walk home. How many students are walking home?
1.Total students = 92.
20 students ride the bus.
So, students not taking the bus = 92 - 20 = 72.
2.5/8 of the remaining students ride bikes.
Number of students riding bikes = (5/8) * 72 = 45.
3.The rest of the students walk home.
Students walking = 72 - 45 = 27.
Conclusion: 27 students walk home.
